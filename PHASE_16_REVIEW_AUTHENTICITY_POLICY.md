## 1. POLICY STATEMENT & ZERO-TOLERANCE COMMITMENT

7Rays Astro Vastu operates under an absolute zero-tolerance standard for fake, fabricated, synthetic, incentivized, or misattributed reviews and testimonials.

Trust is the single most valuable asset in esoteric and architectural consulting. A single manufactured review permanently compromises business credibility, violates Google Business Profile Terms of Service, and breaches advertising regulations established by the Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) and consumer protection statutes.

---

## 2. COMPLIANCE RULES & OPERATIONAL STANDARDS

### 2.1 Genuine Customers & Verifiable Services

- Every public review on Google, Apple, or social profiles, and any testimonial published on `https://7raysastrovastu.com/`, must originate from a **real, living individual or authorized corporate entity** who formally engaged 7Rays Astro Vastu and received a professional consultation.
- Inquiries that did not result in an actual consultation (e.g., price shopping chats, dropped WhatsApp messages) do not qualify as customer relationships.

### 2.2 Strict Prohibition on Fabricated Reviews & Placeholders

- Website developers, content writers, marketing agencies, and consultants are **strictly forbidden** from creating placeholder names, stock headshots, fictitious initialisms, or invented review quotes.
- If no verified reviews have been submitted for a specific new service, the website must either state:  
  _"Client testimonials will be published here as verified feedback becomes available."_ or omit the testimonial section entirely.

### 2.3 Prohibition on Staff, Family & Conflict-of-Interest Reviews

- Employees, family members, personal friends, web developers, or marketing partners are prohibited from reviewing 7Rays Astro Vastu to inflate its rating.
- Google’s algorithmic spam filters detect shared IP networks, matching family surnames, and common device fingerprints, penalizing profiles that attempt self-reviewing.

### 2.4 Review Solicitation Standard (Anti-Gating)

- Review requests must be neutral and voluntary.
- Staff must never ask clients to submit reviews on company-owned devices (e.g. handing a customer an office iPad to leave a 5-star review). Google automatically flags reviews originating from the business's own IP address.
- Customers must leave reviews from their own personal Google accounts on their personal devices.

### 2.5 Incentivization Ban

- It is strictly forbidden to offer:
  - Financial compensation, refunds, or discounts for reviews.
  - Free astrological horoscopes or gemstone recommendations in exchange for 5-star ratings.
  - Entry into lotteries or prize contests conditioned on review submission.

### 2.6 Repeat Clients & Multi-Property Audits

- When a repeat client engages 7Rays for multiple properties (e.g. an apartment in 2024 and an office in 2025), they may update their existing review to reflect their continued experience, rather than creating alternate accounts.

## 3. SUSPECTED FAKE OR DEFAMATORY REVIEWS (AUDIT & TRIAGE)

In the event that an unsolicited, defamatory, or fraudulent review appears on the Google Business Profile:

1. **Never Engage in Public Warfare:** Do not respond with hostility, insults, or threats.
2. **Review Client Database:** Check CRM records to verify if the reviewer was a genuine client.
3. **Professional Response:** If unverified, post the standard non-confrontational verification response (Scenario F from `PHASE_16_REVIEW_RESPONSE_SYSTEM.md`).
4. **Official Google Dispute:** Submit an official removal request via the Google Business Profile Manager selecting _"Conflict of Interest"_ or _"Fake Engagement"_.
5. **Preserve Telemetry:** Retain consultation schedules and communication logs as evidence if Google Support requests documentation.
