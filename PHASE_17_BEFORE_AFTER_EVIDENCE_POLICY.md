### 1. Objective & Scope

Visual before/after comparisons and reported transformations are potent trust signals. However, in holistic spatial advisory (Vastu Shastra), before/after presentations are frequently abused across the industry through fabricated photos, unscientific causation claims, and exaggerated life outcomes.

This policy establishes strict evidentiary boundaries for how "Before / After" conditions are documented, categorized, and published by 7Rays Astro Vastu.

---

### 2. The Three-Layer Separation Rule

Every before/after presentation on the website or marketing materials must strictly isolate three distinct layers of information. They must **never** be conflated:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Layer 1: PHYSICAL & SPATIAL CHANGE                    │
│ (Objective, observable, measurable layout modifications)│
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Layer 2: CLIENT-REPORTED SUBJECTIVE EXPERIENCE         │
│ (Individual feelings, perceptions, self-reported shift)│
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Layer 3: TRADITIONAL VASTU INTERPRETATION              │
│ (Classical symbolic associations and elemental models) │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Layer 1: Physical & Spatial Change (Objective)

- **What it is:** Tangible physical alterations to the space.
- **Acceptable Documentation:**
  - Real architectural layout changes (e.g., repositioning an executive workstation from North-East to South-West).
  - Remedial material installations (e.g., embedding a 99% pure brass energy partition strip flush with the floor tiles).
  - Elemental balancing adjustments (e.g., removing a decorative water feature from the South-East Agni fire zone).
  - Lighting, color temperature, and ventilation modifications.
- **Evidentiary Standard:** Must be substantiated by authentic site photos, CAD markups, or verified installation logs.

#### Layer 2: Client-Reported Subjective Experience (Attributed)

- **What it is:** The client's personal qualitative feedback regarding their comfort, focus, atmosphere, or peace of mind after implementation.
- **Mandatory Attribution Language:**
  - _"The client reported a perceived improvement in workplace tranquility..."_
  - _"According to the homeowner's feedback 60 days post-adjustment..."_
  - _"The occupant expressed greater subjective ease in the renovated study area..."_
- **Strict Prohibition:** Subjective feelings must **never** be cited as empirical or clinical proof of spatial energy transformation.

#### Layer 3: Traditional Vastu Interpretation (Symbolic / Conceptual)

- **What it is:** The philosophical and classical textual rationale behind the recommendation (e.g., Brihat Samhita, Mayamata, Vishwakarma Prakash).
- **Mandatory Attribution Language:**
  - _"In classical Vastu tradition, the North-East (Ishanya) zone is associated with mental clarity and the water element..."_
  - _"According to traditional spatial philosophy, stabilizing the South-West sector represents grounding and leadership energy..."_
- **Strict Prohibition:** Traditional symbolic associations must **never** be phrased as scientific laws, medical cures, or financial certainties.

### 3. Prohibited "Before / After" Practices

The following practices are strictly prohibited across all digital properties of 7Rays Astro Vastu:

1. **Fabricated Outcome Juxtapositions:** Presenting an illustrative or mock floor plan as a "verified before/after client case."
2. **Medical / Health Before & After:** Claiming that repositioning a bed cured chronic illnesses, depression, autoimmune disorders, or clinical pathology.
3. **Financial / Commercial Guarantees:** Claiming that installing a brass boundary strip caused revenue to jump 300% or closed a specific venture round.
4. **Manipulated or Stock Imagery:**
   - Using stock photos of messy vs. tidy rooms to depict Vastu transformation.
   - Using AI-generated interior images to simulate "before" and "after" client estates.
   - Digitally altering lighting, saturation, or gloom in "before" photos to make them look sinister compared to vibrant "after" shots.
5. **Absence of Constraints Disclosure:** Failing to note that client results were influenced by general architectural design, professional management, macroeconomics, or normal lifestyle improvements.

### 4. Mandatory Format for Case Study Interventions

When authentic before/after evidence is approved for publication, it must follow this exact four-part reporting block:

#### 1. Initial State (Before)

- **Spatial Condition:** Explicit description of the existing orientation, zoning conflicts, and directional alignment.
- **Diagnostic Observations:** Measurements recorded (compass degrees, ambient readings, elemental misalignments).
- **Site Constraints:** Immovable structural elements (load-bearing pillars, existing plumbing shafts, leased premises covenants).

#### 2. Intervention (Remedial Action)

- **Specific Methodology:** Exact non-demolition or spatial correction implemented.
- **Materials Used:** Verified physical materials (e.g., pure copper rods, zinc plates, brass strips, lighting alterations).
- **Execution Dates:** Month and year of installation.

#### 3. Observable Spatial Outcome (Physical)

- **Verified Adjustments:** Layout verification confirming correct placement, flush fitting, and zero structural disruption.

#### 4. Qualitative Observation & Disclaimer

- **Client Remarks:** Quoted or summarized subjective feedback with explicit consent.
- **Disclaimer:**
  > _"Observations reflect individualized client feedback and traditional Vastu spatial alignment. Vastu recommendations are complementary spatial advisory services and do not guarantee business revenues, financial returns, medical recovery, or life events."_
