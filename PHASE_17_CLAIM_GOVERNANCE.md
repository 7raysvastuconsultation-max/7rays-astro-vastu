### 2. Absolute Prohibitions (Never Permitted)

Under no circumstances may 7Rays Astro Vastu claim, imply, suggest, or advertise that Vastu Shastra, non-demolition remedies, or astrological consultations are **scientifically proven** to produce specific life outcomes.

#### Prohibited Outcome Guarantees:

1. **Medical & Health Guarantees:**
   - Curing diseases, chronic medical ailments, or genetic conditions.
   - Eliminating depression, anxiety, insomnia, or psychological disorders without clinical care.
   - Guaranteeing pregnancy, child conception, gender selection, or fertility.
   - Prolonging human life expectancy or preventing death.
2. **Financial & Investment Guarantees:**
   - Guaranteeing business profit increases, EBITDA expansion, or startup valuations.
   - Guaranteeing investment returns, stock market windfalls, or lottery wins.
   - Promising debt liquidation, bankruptcy avoidance, or recovery of lost funds.
3. **Personal & Career Guarantees:**
   - Guaranteeing marriage, romantic reconciliation, or divorce prevention.
   - Guaranteeing employment offers, corporate promotions, or competitive exam clearance.
   - Guaranteeing litigation dismissal or favorable judicial verdicts.

#### Prohibited Phrasing Patterns:

- ❌ _"Scientifically proven to eliminate financial stress."_
- ❌ _"Guaranteed 100% Vastu results without demolition."_
- ❌ _"Remedies that cure chronic health issues in 21 days."_
- ❌ _"Miracle Astro-Vastu alignment for guaranteed wealth."_
- ❌ _"Energy corrections proven by modern physics to multiply revenues."_
