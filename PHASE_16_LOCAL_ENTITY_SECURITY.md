## 3. MANDATORY SECURITY PROTOCOLS

### 3.1 Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

- The Google Account managing Google Business Profile, the Apple ID managing Apple Business Connect, and the Microsoft Account managing Bing Places must have **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** enabled using an authenticator app (Google Authenticator) or security hardware key.
- Never rely exclusively on single-password access.

### 3.2 Anti-Hijacking Monitoring

Competitors or malicious third parties occasionally attempt to "Suggest an Edit" on Google Maps to alter phone numbers, mark a business as "Permanently Closed", or change the official website link.

- **Protocol:** The owner will receive an automated email from Google Business Profile whenever an external edit is suggested.
- **Action:** Reject any unauthorized suggested edits immediately within 48 hours to prevent Google’s automated system from accepting community edits by default.

### 3.3 Ownership Claim Requests

If an unauthorized third party clicks "Claim this business" on Google Maps:

- Google will send an email notification to the Primary Owner stating that someone has requested management access.
- **Action:** Click **REJECT** immediately. Never ignore ownership request emails from Google.

### 3.4 Recovery Information Hygiene

- Ensure the recovery email and recovery mobile phone linked to the primary Google Business Profile account are owned exclusively by Rishwa Sinha and not tied to temporary agency staff or former contractors.
