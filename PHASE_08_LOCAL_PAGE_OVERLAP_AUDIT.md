## 1. Local Architecture Principles & Doorway Policy

To adhere to Google Search Central's guidance on doorway pages and local landing pages:

1. **Single Physical Location Policy**: 7Rays Astro Vastu has **only ONE physical business address**: `3J64+827, Balaji Layout, H A Farm Post, Bhuvaneswari Nagar, Dasarahalli, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560024`.
2. **Zero Fictitious Branch Offices**: The website does not, and must never, claim physical branches in Indiranagar, HSR Layout, Koramangala, Whitefield, or any other micro-market.
3. **Explicit Service Area Definition**: Neighborhood pages represent _on-site property inspection service territories_ (`areaServed`), not brick-and-mortar storefronts.
4. **Anti-Doorway Standard**: Every local URL must offer substantive, non-templated content addressing genuine architectural, infrastructural, or zoning traits of that locality. If two pages are substantially interchangeable, they must be consolidated.

## 3. Consolidation & Anti-Expansion Recommendation

1. **Strict Neighborhood Cap:** The 4 existing neighborhood pages (`indiranagar`, `hsr-layout`, `koramangala`, `whitefield`) represent the major commercial/residential hubs of Bangalore with distinct architectural characteristics.
2. **DO NOT Expand to Additional Neighborhoods:** Do NOT create standalone pages for Jayanagar, JP Nagar, Malleshwaram, Rajajinagar, Hebbal, Yelahanka, Electronic City, or Sarjapur. Creating template-based pages for these localities would cross the threshold into Google Doorway Page violations.
3. **Consolidation Pattern:** All future locality queries should be satisfied via:
   - Topic sections on `/locations/bangalore`
   - Location mentions in relevant case studies and blog articles
   - The verified service areas array in `businessConfig` (`areaServed`).
