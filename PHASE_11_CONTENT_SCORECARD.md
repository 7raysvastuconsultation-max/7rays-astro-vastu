## 1. Internal QA Evaluation Criteria (1–10 Scale)

1. **Intent Satisfaction (IS):** Directly and completely fulfills the user's primary query before introducing secondary topics.
2. **Information Gain (IG):** Introduces original frameworks, unique tables, or practical insights absent on generic competing sites.
3. **Originality (OR):** Avoids generic AI boilerplate; presents fresh spatial perspectives and non-demolition realism.
4. **Expertise (EX):** Reflects 5+ years of verified consulting experience and traditional textual grounding.
5. **Trust & Governance (TG):** Strict compliance with claims policy (no health cures, wealth guarantees, or fake reviews).
6. **Practical Usefulness (PU):** Provides actionable blueprints, compass degree rules, and concrete advice.
7. **Topical Completeness (TC):** Thoroughly covers the core entity and adjacent sub-topics without keyword fluff.
8. **Local Relevance (LR):** Contextualizes advice for Bangalore's architecture, lease rules, and geography.
9. **AEO Readiness (AEO):** Direct-answer summaries, clear definitions, and question-oriented headings for AI extraction.
10. **Internal Topic Graph (ITG):** Balanced upward, sideways, local, and conversion linking.
11. **Conversion Clarity (CC):** Clear, low-pressure pathways to book a consultation or view deliverables.
12. **Content Freshness (CF):** Reviewed and updated to reflect 2026 industry standards and verified business facts.
