## 3. WHAT NOT TO "FIX" (NON-DAMAGING VARIATIONS)

To maximize operational efficiency, team members and owners must **never** waste time attempting to standardize the following harmless variations:

1. **Linguistic City Name:** "Bengaluru" vs "Bangalore" (Both resolve to the same geo-entity in search databases).
2. **Phone Number Spacing:** `+91 70910 21616` vs `+917091021616` vs `070910 21616` (All map to the same telecommunication endpoint).
3. **Street Descriptor Case:** "Balaji Layout" vs "Balaji Lyt" (Standard postal abbreviations are parsed natively by mapping engines).
4. **Country Code Inclusion:** Address listings that omit "India" when published on Indian national directories (country context is implied by national TLDs).
