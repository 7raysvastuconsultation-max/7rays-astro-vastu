## 1. CORE OPERATIONAL ETHICS & ANTI-INCENTIVIZATION POLICY

The review generation system of 7Rays Astro Vastu is strictly grounded in genuine professional engagements. Google, the Federal Trade Commission (FTC), and the Indian Consumer Protection Act strictly prohibit fake, purchased, or gated customer reviews.

### Strict Ethical Mandates:

1. **Never Purchase Reviews:** Zero third-party review buying services, review swaps, or click-farm engagement.
2. **Never Review-Gate:** Never ask clients "Were you happy with us? If yes, click Google; if no, fill a private form." All clients must receive an equal, direct link to leave their unedited feedback.
3. **Never Script Customer Words:** Never supply pre-written reviews or suggest specific keywords (e.g., "Please write: Rishwa Sinha is the best Vastu consultant in Bangalore"). Customers must speak purely from their authentic experience.
4. **Never Incentivize Ratings:** Zero discounts, free gifts, cashback, or future consultation perks in exchange for 5-star ratings.
5. **No Employee or Family Reviews:** Reviews must come exclusively from verified clients who engaged 7Rays for a genuine consultation.
