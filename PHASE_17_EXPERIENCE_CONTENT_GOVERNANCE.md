### 1. Purpose & Core Governance Philosophy

To maintain unimpeachable E-E-A-T credibility with search engines, AI answer engines, and human visitors, every substantive statement, claim, methodology description, or anecdotal reference published by 7Rays Astro Vastu must be auditable under a rigorous **Evidentiary Classification Taxonomy**.

Content writers, editors, developers, and AI agents must classify every narrative statement into one of six distinct categories. Any statement falling into the unverified category must be immediately edited, verified, or expunged.

### 3. Detailed Classification Rules & Attribution Standards

#### Class 1: Verified First-Hand Experience

- **Definition:** Specific insights, site observations, or field experiences directly encountered by Rishwa Sinha during real consultations.
- **Permitted Phrases:**
  - _"In our on-site assessments across Bengaluru..."_
  - _"During multi-story apartment inspections, we consistently observe..."_
  - _"In practical application, we have found that..."_
- **Evidentiary Requirement:** Must be grounded in genuine field experience; verifiable by Rishwa upon request.

#### Class 2: Owner-Verified Methodology

- **Definition:** Formal, systematic procedures, diagnostic tools, and materials officially verified as part of the 7Rays service offering.
- **Verified Assets:** Calibrated digital compass, digital Gauss meter, traditional dowsing rods, CAD 16-zone spatial zoning, metallic inlays (brass, copper, zinc, lead), Parashari Jyotish birth chart analysis.
- **Permitted Phrases:**
  - _"Our assessment protocol utilizes..."_
  - _"7Rays applies non-demolition boundary rectifications using..."_
  - _"We determine true geographic orientation using calibrated digital instruments..."_
- **Evidentiary Requirement:** Tools and methods must physically exist in the practice and be regularly employed.

#### Class 3: General Educational Knowledge

- **Definition:** Theoretical, historical, or classical concepts derived from established Vedic texts (Mayamata, Manasara, Brihat Samhita) or general building sciences.
- **Permitted Phrases:**
  - _"According to classical Vastu tradition..."_
  - _"Traditional texts associate the South-East quadrant with the fire element (Agni)..."_
  - _"In architectural building physics, cross-ventilation improves thermal comfort..."_
- **Evidentiary Requirement:** Must cite or reference recognized classical traditions or architectural facts without claiming original invention.

#### Class 4: Client-Reported Experience

- **Definition:** Anecdotal, subjective feedback, emotional responses, or observations communicated by clients following recommendations.
- **Permitted Phrases:**
  - _"Clients frequently report feeling a greater sense of spatial balance..."_
  - _"Homeowners note perceived improvements in household harmony..."_
  - _"According to client feedback following spatial realignment..."_
- **Evidentiary Requirement:** Must be clearly attributed as subjective perception and accompanied by the standard non-guarantee disclaimer.

#### Class 5: Illustrative / Educational Scenario

- **Definition:** Hypothetical or composite case studies created solely for instructional purposes to demonstrate how Vastu principles apply to common layouts.
- **Mandatory Labeling:** Must feature the prominent badge: **"Illustrative Vastu Assessment"** or **"Educational Scenario"** alongside the amber disclaimer banner:
  > _"This is an educational scenario demonstrating spatial assessment methodology, not a real client case study. No personal or commercial outcomes are guaranteed."_
- **Evidentiary Requirement:** Must never use real client names, fake company identities, or fabricated quantitative outcomes.

#### Class 6: Unverified — Remove or Verify

- **Definition:** Any statement that lacks factual substantiation, claims unverified credentials, fabricates statistical figures, or promises specific life/financial/health results.
- **Action Required:**
  - **IMMEDIATE REMOVAL** if the claim involves health cures, wealth guarantees, or fake statistics.
  - **OWNER VERIFICATION QUEUE** if the claim refers to a real potential project or credential that simply needs documentation.

### 4. Ongoing Content Maintenance & Audit Workflow

1. **Pre-Publishing Review:** Every line of newly proposed copy must be mapped to Classes 1–5. Zero Class 6 text may be merged into production code.
2. **Periodic Codebase Scans:** Automated and manual keyword grep sweeps for trigger words (_"proven", "guaranteed", "cure", "100%", "studies show"_) must run during quality reviews.
3. **Escalation Protocol:** Any ambiguity regarding whether an experience statement is Class 1 or Class 6 must be resolved by querying Rishwa Sinha via the Owner Verification Queue.
