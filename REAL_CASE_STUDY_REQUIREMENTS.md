# 7Rays Astro Vastu — Genuine Case Study Evidence Standard

## Mandatory Verification Protocol for Publishing Real Client Engagements

**Policy Effective Date:** September 2026  
**Governing Standard:** E-E-A-T Authenticity, Consumer Protection, and Advertising Truthfulness  
**Applies To:** All content published under `/case-studies/*` or cited across marketing surfaces.

---

### 1. Core Principle

**Hypothetical scenarios, composite examples, and educational walkthroughs must NEVER be labeled as real client case studies.**

Unless an engagement meets 100% of the mandatory verification criteria below, it must be prominently designated as:

- **"Illustrative Vastu Assessment"**, or
- **"Example Consultation Scenario"**

---

Before any client project can be published as a genuine "Case Study" on `7raysastrovastu.com`, the following 10 evidence items must be on file:

| **1**  | **Client Written Consent**                 | Signed consent form or explicit written approval (email/letter) authorizing public documentation of the project.                              | **YES (Absolute)**           |

---

### 3. Prohibited Case Study Practices

1. **NO Fabricated Persona Details:** Never invent fake client names, initials, company names, or stock-photo headshots.
2. **NO Manufactured Commercial Metrics:** Never claim financial or business transformations (e.g., _"startup secured Series A in 3 weeks"_, _"store sales surged by 45%"_) unless audited, certified, and authorized by the client in writing.
3. **NO Manufactured Health Cures:** Never state or imply that spatial changes cured medical pathologies, chronic diseases, or mental health disorders.
4. **NO Testimonial Extrapolation:** Quotes must be verbatim from authenticated communications.
