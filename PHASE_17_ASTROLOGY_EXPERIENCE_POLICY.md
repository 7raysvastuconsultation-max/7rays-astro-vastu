### 3. Permitted Framing & Conceptual Language

Astrology must always be presented as an introspective, philosophical, and traditional system of timing and self-awareness, rather than an infallible, deterministic prophecy engine.

#### Permitted Terms & Concepts:

- **Classical Vedic Astrology (Jyotish Shastra):** The foundational Indian astrological tradition based on the sidereal zodiac and Nakshatra system.
- **Parashari Jyotish Framework:** The classical Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra principles utilized for planetary dignity, house rulership, and Dasha analysis.
- **Traditional Chart Interpretation (Kundali / Janam Patri):** Analyzing the natal birth chart as a diagnostic map of innate tendencies, strengths, and life cycles.
- **Planetary Timing (Dasha & Gochara):** Evaluating major and sub-cycles (Vimshottari Dasha) and planetary transits to identify favorable windows for decision-making and patience.
- **Self-Reflection & Mindset Alignment:** Framing astrological insights as tools for introspection, behavioral self-correction, emotional resilience, and intentional planning.
- **Ethical Gemstone & Remedial Approach:** Recommending gemstone or charity guidance purely within classical traditions, never claiming gemstones will physically alter external reality or guarantee wealth.
