**Independence:** GSC-Independent / Anti-Fabrication Standard

---

### 1. Purpose of the Publishing Gate

The integrity of 7Rays Astro Vastu depends upon never misleading search engines or consumers with synthetic or exaggerated case studies.

This **Case Study Publishing Gate** is a mandatory, auditable checklist that every prospective project write-up must pass before it can be published as a "Real Client Case Study" or added to the website, portfolio, or promotional media.

If even **one** required gate condition fails, the project **CANNOT** be published as a real case study. It must either be remediated or labeled strictly as an **Educational Scenario**.

---

### 2. The 11-Point Mandatory Publishing Gate

```
================================================================================
           7RAYS ASTRO VASTU — CASE STUDY PRE-PUBLISHING GATE
================================================================================
Project Ref Code: _____________________    Review Date: ________________________
Assessor / Author: Rishwa Sinha            Reviewer: ___________________________
================================================================================
```

| Check # | Requirement                             | Verification Standard                                                                                                                                                                                                |       Pass / Fail       |
| :-----: | :-------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------: |
| **01**  | **Real Verified Project**               | The consultation actually took place with a real person or entity. Consultation records, dates, and communications exist in company archives.                                                                        | `[ ] PASS` / `[ ] FAIL` |
| **02**  | **Documentary Evidence Exists**         | Physical or digital evidence of the work exists: initial floor plan, compass measurements, CAD zoning markups, or written recommendation report.                                                                     | `[ ] PASS` / `[ ] FAIL` |
| **03**  | **Client Consent Executed**             | Signed Client Consent Form on file corresponding to Tier 1, 2, 3, or 4 consent (anonymous, generic regional, verified named, or visual).                                                                             | `[ ] PASS` / `[ ] FAIL` |
| **04**  | **Factual Claims Audited**              | All claims regarding property size, directional orientation, defects identified, and corrections recommended are factually verified against the assessment file.                                                     | `[ ] PASS` / `[ ] FAIL` |
| **05**  | **Zero Fabricated Outcomes**            | No financial gains (revenue %, investment raised), medical cures, or guaranteed life transformations are stated or implied. Only documented physical changes and attributed subjective client feedback are included. | `[ ] PASS` / `[ ] FAIL` |
| **06**  | **Testimonial Authenticity**            | Any client quotation included is verbatim, dated, unedited in substance, and explicitly authorized for publication by the client.                                                                                    | `[ ] PASS` / `[ ] FAIL` |
| **07**  | **Privacy & Redaction Review**          | All sensitive details (full house numbers, street addresses, family photos, employer names, confidential architectural dimensions) are completely scrubbed or blurred.                                               | `[ ] PASS` / `[ ] FAIL` |
| **08**  | **Authentic Visual Imagery**            | Photographs or CAD diagrams used are original from the actual project. No stock photos or AI-generated scenes masquerading as project photos.                                                                        | `[ ] PASS` / `[ ] FAIL` |
| **09**  | **Author Attributed**                   | Author is explicitly identified as Rishwa Sinha, Certified Vastu Consultant, with professional role and oversight clearly documented.                                                                                | `[ ] PASS` / `[ ] FAIL` |
| **10**  | **Timeline & Dates Documented**         | Consultation date, implementation period, and follow-up timeline are clearly stated (e.g., "Consulted: March 2025; Follow-up: June 2025").                                                                           | `[ ] PASS` / `[ ] FAIL` |
| **11**  | **Limitations & Disclaimers Disclosed** | Explicit statement disclosing that outcomes represent individual client feedback, that traditional principles were applied, and that Vastu does not guarantee financial or health results.                           | `[ ] PASS` / `[ ] FAIL` |
