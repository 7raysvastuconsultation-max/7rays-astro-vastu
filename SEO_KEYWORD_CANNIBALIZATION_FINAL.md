## 1. Executive Summary & Cannibalization Risk Spectrum

Keyword cannibalization occurs when two or more URLs on the same domain compete for identical or substantially overlapping search queries, splitting organic click-through rates and confusing search engine ranking models.

In spatial consultancy websites, cannibalization frequently arises between:

1. General service hubs vs. specialized property sub-pages (e.g. Residential vs. Apartment).
2. General commercial hubs vs. office/workplace pages.
3. Universal service pages vs. city-specific service pages (e.g. `/vastu/residential` vs. `/locations/bangalore/residential-vastu`).
4. Universal Vastu audits vs. plot/construction checks.

The following audit dissects the 7 primary potential conflict pairs identified across the 59 canonical routes and enforces definitive intent boundaries.

### Conflict Pair 1: General Residential Vastu vs. Apartment & Flat Vastu

- **PAGE A:** `https://7raysastrovastu.in/vastu/residential`
- **PAGE B:** `https://7raysastrovastu.in/vastu/apartment-vastu`
- **OVERLAPPING INTENT:** Home Vastu consultation, residential flat layout analysis.
- **WHY THEY OVERLAP:** Both pages address domestic living spaces. High-rise apartment buyers often search for generic "home Vastu" or "residential Vastu" without realizing the distinct physical constraints of apartments compared to independent villas.
- **WHICH PAGE SHOULD OWN WHICH INTENT:**
  - **PAGE A (`/vastu/residential`):** Owns broad residential intent (`residential Vastu consultant`, `house Vastu Bangalore`, `independent villa Vastu`, `duplex home Vastu planning`). Focuses on independent land boundaries, multi-level living, private water tanks, and unconstrained structural planning.
  - **PAGE B (`/vastu/apartment-vastu`):** Owns specialized multi-family urban intent (`apartment Vastu Bangalore`, `high-rise flat Vastu remedies`, `leased flat Vastu`, `3BHK apartment Vastu`). Focuses on fixed structural columns, shared plumbing ducts, pre-cast walls, balcony directions, and common corridor entrance restrictions.
- **RECOMMENDED DIFFERENTIATION:**
  - Page A emphasizes macro-level property orientation, garden/compound slopes, and ground-up architectural layouts.
  - Page B emphasizes micro-level internal spatial reallocation, non-demolition metallic inlays, and balancing fixed builder layouts.
- **INTERNAL LINKING SOLUTION:**
  - On Page A: Include a prominent card: _"Living in a high-rise condominium? Explore our specialized [Apartment & Flat Vastu Consultation](/vastu/apartment-vastu) designed for fixed structural layouts."_
  - On Page B: Include a breadcrumb and contextual link back to `/vastu/residential` for clients considering independent villas.
- **TITLE / H1 STATUS:**
  - Page A Title: `Residential Vastu Consultant in Bangalore | 7Rays` | H1: `Residential Vastu Consultation`
  - Page B Title: `Apartment & Flat Vastu Consultation Bangalore | 7Rays` | H1: `Apartment & High-Rise Flat Vastu Consultation`
