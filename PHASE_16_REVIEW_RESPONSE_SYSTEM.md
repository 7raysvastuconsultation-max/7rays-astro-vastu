## 1. STRATEGIC RESPONSE PHILOSOPHY

Google Business Profile review responses are read as much by prospective future clients as by the reviewer. How 7Rays responds establishes its brand authority, professional composure, and client care.

### Core Writing Principles:

1. **Human & Authentic:** Never use robotic, auto-generated boilerplate or copy-pasted corporate lines across multiple reviews.
2. **Strict Privacy Shield:** Never confirm confidential details publicly (e.g., specific apartment unit numbers, financial stress, marital issues, or legal property disputes).
3. **No Keyword Stuffing:** Never hijack responses with unnatural phrases like _"Thank you for calling 7Rays the best Vastu consultant in Bangalore for residential Vastu in Whitefield"_. Search engines penalize obvious keyword stuffed responses, and high-net-worth clients find them tacky.
4. **Prompt Accountability:** Respond to all reviews within 24 to 48 hours of publication.
