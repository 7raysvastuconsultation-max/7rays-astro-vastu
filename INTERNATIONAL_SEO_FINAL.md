## 1. Executive Summary & Policy on Country Pages

A common mistake in international SEO is generating dozens of near-identical country pages (e.g. `/vastu-consultant-usa`, `/vastu-consultant-uk`, `/vastu-consultant-dubai`). This practice creates doorway pages that dilute domain authority and risk penalties under Google's Spam Policies.

**7Rays Astro Vastu** adopts a centralized, authority-first international architecture:

- **Primary International Gateway:** `https://7raysastrovastu.in/international`
- **ccTLD Authority:** Operates from the authentic Indian country-code top-level domain (`.in`), establishing authoritative origin for traditional Vedic knowledge that overseas diaspora audiences actively seek.
- **Unified Remote Process:** Targets international and NRI search queries through a single comprehensive landing page addressing global CAD blueprint analysis, satellite orientation, and multi-timezone video consultations.

## 4. International Expansion Policy (Post-Launch)

Country-specific sub-pages (e.g. `/international/usa` or `/international/uae`) will **ONLY** be created if:

1. Physical local representation, a local phone number, or localized currency checkout (Stripe/PayPal in USD/AED) is established.
2. Search Console data demonstrates substantial country-specific query impressions that justify a unique landing page with localized customer case studies.
3. Every new country page contains 100% unique, non-templated copy reflecting local building codes.

Until those criteria are met, the centralized `/international` hub remains the most mathematically sound, penalty-safe strategy for global search acquisition.
