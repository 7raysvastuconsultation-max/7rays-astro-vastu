## 1. Core Principles of Review Responses

1. **Promptness:** Respond to all reviews (positive, neutral, negative) within **24–48 hours**.
2. **Confidentiality & Privacy Protection:**
   - **Never** state the client's home apartment number, street address, or personal financial details in a public response.
   - **Never** discuss confidential personal life circumstances, marital friction, or specific birth chart vulnerabilities in public replies.
3. **No Keyword Stuffing:** Avoid unnatural robotic sentences like _"Thank you for calling the best Vastu consultant in Bangalore for your flat Vastu in Bangalore"_. Keep responses gracious, warm, and authentic.
4. **De-escalation First:** Never argue, blame, or act defensively against critical feedback.
