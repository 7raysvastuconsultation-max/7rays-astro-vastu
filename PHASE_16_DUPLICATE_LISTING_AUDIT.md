## 1. DUPLICATE LISTING AUDIT METHODOLOGY

Duplicate listings dilute local authority, split customer reviews across multiple URLs, confuse map navigation algorithms, and trigger algorithmic suspension on Google Business Profile.

We audited for six potential categories of local duplication:

1. **Name Variations:** Listings operating under variants like "7 Rays Vastu", "7Rays Astrology", or "Seven Rays Vastu Consultancy".
2. **Address Duplicates:** Multiple listings registered to the Dasarahalli address or residential units in Balaji Layout.
3. **Ghost / Virtual Branches:** Unauthorized or historical listings in Indiranagar, Whitefield, Koramangala, or HSR Layout without physical premises.
4. **Phone Duplication:** Listings sharing the primary contact phone `+91 70910 21616` under another consultant's name.
5. **Personal Practitioner vs. Brand Conflicts:** Unclaimed personal practitioner cards competing with the brand entity.
6. **Third-Party Auto-Generated Scrapes:** Automated directory scrapers (Justdial, Sulekha) creating unverified placeholder profiles from public records.
