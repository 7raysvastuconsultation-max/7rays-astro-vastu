## 1. CLASSIFICATION TAXONOMY

Each local ecosystem directory and listing is systematically audited and classified into one of eight distinct operational categories:

- **Class A — VERIFIED:** Listing is active, live, publicly discoverable, and matches the canonical business identity 100%.
- **Class B — OWNER ACCESS REQUIRED:** Listing exists or has an official claim pathway requiring direct owner verification (phone OTP, postal postcard, or domain email authentication).
- **Class C — NOT YET CLAIMED:** Platform is legitimate, relevant, and authoritative, but profile creation has not yet been initiated by the business owner.
- **Class D — NEEDS CORRECTION:** Listing exists but contains outdated or inconsistent NAP data (e.g., missing Plus Code, old phone, missing website).
- **Class E — DUPLICATE:** Multiple listings exist on the same platform representing the same physical location.
- **Class F — IRRELEVANT:** Platform has zero local relevance, no Bangalore presence, or serves an incompatible vertical (e.g. general ecommerce, foreign medical directories).
- **Class G — POTENTIALLY RISKY:** Automated link farm, low-quality submission directory, or spam network that could incur algorithmic link penalties.
- **Class H — UNKNOWN / NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Listing presence cannot be definitively confirmed without owner dashboard access.
