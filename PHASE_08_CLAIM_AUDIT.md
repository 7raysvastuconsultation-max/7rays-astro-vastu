## 1. Classification Framework

Every claim found in the codebase is categorized into one of four strict classifications:

- **A. Verified business service/methodology:** Directly backed by `businessConfig` (`src/config/business.ts`), authentic core business offerings led by Rishwa Sinha, and verifiable operational facts.
- **B. Educational/classical Vastu concept:** Grounded in classical canonical Vedic literature (_Manasara_, _Mayamata_, _Brihat Samhita_, _Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra_). Published strictly as traditional philosophy and educational knowledge without empirical medical or financial guarantees.
- **C. Requires owner verification:** Plausible consulting methodologies, specific physical instrumentation, or operational turnaround metrics that require explicit owner sign-off from Rishwa Sinha before entering binding contracts or paid promotion.
- **D. Unsupported — remove/rewrite:** Speculative, scientifically unproven, miraculous, medical, or unverified statistical claims that risk Google Quality Rater / YMYL penalties and must be removed or rewritten with responsible editorial qualifications.
