## 1. Prioritization Methodology

Each page is evaluated on a 7-factor weighted scoring model (Score 1–5 per dimension, Max 35 points):

1. **Business Importance (BI):** Revenue and brand positioning impact.
2. **Search Intent Alignment (SIA):** Precision of satisfying user search goals without commercial friction.
3. **Organic Opportunity (OO):** Potential to capture high-intent informational and transactional queries.
4. **Topical Authority (TA):** Core pillar depth and cluster anchor status.
5. **Information Gap (IG):** Degree to which existing page lacks unique frameworks or practical decision tools.
6. **Conversion Relevance (CR):** Directness of connection to consultation booking.
7. **Local Relevance (LR):** Bangalore geographic and architectural specificity.

### Priority Tiers

- **Tier 1 (Priority P0 / P1 — Score 30–35):** Immediate implementation in Phase 11.
- **Tier 2 (Priority P2 — Score 25–29):** High-impact structural & topical linking enhancements.
- **Tier 3 (Priority P3 / P4 — Score < 25):** Secondary refinements and ongoing maintenance.
