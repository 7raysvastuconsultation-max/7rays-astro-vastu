Search engines, evaluation guidelines, and prospective clients increasingly discount generic stock photos and synthetic AI imagery. Authentic, unvarnished visual evidence establishes undeniable proof of:

1. Physical existence and local presence in Bengaluru.

### 2. Strict Photography Authenticity Rules

To maintain absolute integrity across Google Search, Google Business Profile, and the website:

1. **Zero Stock Masquerading:** Stock photographs may remain for atmospheric/lifestyle background elements, but must **never** be labeled, captioned, or structured in schema as "our team," "our office," "our consultation," or "on-site assessment."
2. **Zero Synthetic / AI Generation for Proof:** AI-generated images of people holding compasses, fake floor plans, or synthetic office interiors are strictly prohibited as evidence.
3. **Zero Staged Fraud:** Do not hire actors to pose as fake clients or stage mock handshakes in rented conference rooms presented as long-term client engagements.
4. **Privacy Protection:** Any authentic client site photos must have all personal identifiers (family portraits, certificates, company logos, computer monitors) thoroughly blurred or excluded from framing.

### 4. Technical Specifications for Owner Capture

To ensure optimal web performance and Core Web Vitals (CWV) compliance:

- **Camera / Device:** Modern smartphone (iPhone 13+ / Samsung Galaxy S21+ / Google Pixel 6+) or DSLR/mirrorless camera in natural daylight.
- **Aspect Ratios:**
  - Landscape: `16:9` or `4:3` (for desktop heroes, methodology breakdowns).
  - Portrait: `3:4` or `4:5` (for author bio, mobile cards).
  - Square: `1:1` (for avatar icons, GBP post images).
- **Lighting:** Bright, indirect natural light; avoid harsh flash reflections off polished brass, copper, or computer screens.
- **File Format & Delivery:** Raw high-res JPEG/PNG uploaded to designated owner staging folder. The technical pipeline will automatically convert them to responsive WebP with WebP fallback, optimized `srcset`, and descriptive alt text.
