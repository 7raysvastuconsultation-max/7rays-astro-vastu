## 3. Evidentiary Guardrails for Content Creation

1. **Attribution Prefixing:** Every metaphysical or traditional rule must be prefixed with:
   - _"According to traditional Vastu principles..."_
   - _"Within classical Vedic astrology (Jyotisha)..."_
   - _"7Rays' consulting methodology emphasizes..."_
2. **Physical Measurement Boundaries:** Technical readings from digital compasses and Gauss meters must be described accurately as directional orientation degrees and electromagnetic field readings (mG / µT), never as "mystical aura scans".
3. **No Fabricated Proof:** When real client outcomes are not accompanied by explicit written consent, the content MUST remain labeled as an _"Illustrative Vastu Assessment"_ or _"Consultation Scenario"_.
