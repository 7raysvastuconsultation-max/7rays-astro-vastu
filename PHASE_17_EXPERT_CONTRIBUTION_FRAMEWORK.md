### 1. Objective & Purpose

To elevate the E-E-A-T standing of 7Rays Astro Vastu, Rishwa Sinha's demonstrated experience must be systematically integrated across internal site content and external publications.

This framework establishes strict verification protocols for how Rishwa's expert commentary is collected, attributed, and distributed, ensuring that every published insight reflects genuine first-hand professional judgment.

---

### 2. The Strict Anti-Ghostwriting Rule

In accordance with genuine E-E-A-T standards:

- **No Fabricated Quotations:** Copywriters, developers, and SEO agents must **never** invent synthetic quotes, fabricate personal anecdotes, or invent past client stories and attribute them to Rishwa Sinha.
- **Owner-Approved Voice:** Any quotation, expert tip, or named contribution presented as a direct quote from Rishwa Sinha must either:
  1. Be transcribed directly from an audio/written interview with Rishwa, or
  2. Be submitted to Rishwa via the Owner Verification Queue and explicitly approved in writing prior to publication.

### 4. Workflow for Capturing Expert Commentary

To ensure high-frequency, authentic expert output without placing excessive administrative burden on Rishwa, the following four-step capture process is established:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Step 1: Prompt Submission                              │
│ Technical agent prepares 3 targeted, open-ended        │
│ questions regarding a specific practical challenge.   │
└───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                            │
┌───────────────────────────▼────────────────────────────┐
│ Step 2: Audio / Voice Memo Capture                     │
│ Rishwa records a 2-to-3-minute unscripted audio reply   │
│ sharing actual field observations and professional view│
└───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                            │
┌───────────────────────────▼────────────────────────────┐
│ Step 3: Transcription & Editorial Polish               │
│ Audio is transcribed, grammar polished, and formatted   │
│ into a cohesive excerpt while preserving the voice.   │
└───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                            │
┌───────────────────────────▼────────────────────────────┐
│ Step 4: Final Verification & Publishing                │
│ Rishwa reviews the written text, confirms accuracy,     │
│ and signs off for publication.                         │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
