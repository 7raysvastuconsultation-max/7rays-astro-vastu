## 3. REVIEW HEALTH & RESPONSE AUDIT CHECKLIST

Each week, the consultation coordinator reviews this tracker against the following benchmarks:

- [ ] **100% Response Rate:** Have all reviews received an individual, personalized response?
- [ ] **Response SLA:** Were all responses published within 48 hours of review posting?
- [ ] **Zero Keyword Stuffing:** Are all responses written in natural, gracious human language without manipulative SEO phrases?
- [ ] **Zero Confirmed Private Data:** Do responses strictly avoid disclosing specific apartment units or sensitive client life details?
- [ ] **Dispute Resolution:** Have any 1-star or 2-star entries been contacted directly via phone or WhatsApp to offer personal review and resolution?
