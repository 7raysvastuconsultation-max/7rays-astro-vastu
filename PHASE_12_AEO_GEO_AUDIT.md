### Audit Framework & Evaluation Legend

Each URL is assessed across 28 criteria:

1. **Primary Intent** (Informational / Commercial Investigation / Transactional / Local / Navigational)
2. **Main Question(s) Answered** (Primary search query in natural question format)
3. **Secondary Questions** (Follow-up consumer questions)
4. **Direct-Answer Availability** (Is a clear, concise direct answer present?)
5. **Answer Placement** (Top-of-fold, under H1, in dedicated section, or buried)
6. **Definition Clarity** (Are core concepts defined explicitly?)
7. **Heading Question Coverage** (Are H2/H3 headings framed as natural questions?)
8. **Extractable Paragraphs** (Self-contained 40–60 word answer units)
9. **Lists & Bullet Clarity** (Structured scannable criteria)
10. **Structured Tables** (Comparative or relational data matrices)
11. **FAQs** (Presence and quality of structured FAQ content)
12. **Entity Clarity** (7Rays Astro Vastu, Vastu Shastra, Vedic Astrology)
13. **Person Attribution** (Rishwa Sinha, Certified Vastu Consultant)
14. **Organization Attribution** (7Rays Astro Vastu entity mapping)
15. **Local Context** (Dasarahalli, Bengaluru 560024 headquarters)
16. **Internal Semantic Relationships** (Upward, sideways, and local connections)
17. **Supporting Content Links** (Connected cluster guides)
18. **Source / Tradition Citation** (Classical Vedic texts: _Mayamatam_, _Samarangana Sutradhara_, _Brihat Samhita_)
19. **AEO Readiness Score** (Scale 1–10)
20. **AI-Search Readability** (How easily can LLMs parse the page hierarchy?)
21. **Human Readability** (Clarity, flow, and visual elegance)
22. **Content Uniqueness** (Original frameworks vs. generic web content)
23. **Information Gain** (Non-demolition physics, metallic inlays, calibrated compass)
24. **Conversion Relevance** (Clear, low-pressure path to consultation)
25. **Schema Support** (BreadcrumbList, Service, LocalBusiness, FAQPage, Article)
26. **Risks of Ambiguity** (Potential misinterpretations by search algorithms)
27. **Missing Context** (Unaddressed consumer background needs)
28. **Missing Limitations** (Disclaimers on civil engineering, lease terms, non-medical status)

**Classification Codes:**

- `AEO-READY`: Satisfies all direct answer, extractability, and entity standards.
- `AEO-IMPROVEMENT`: High-value page requiring targeted direct-answer, question heading, or table refinement.
- `NEEDS-MAJOR-RESTRUCTURE`: Severe architectural or semantic deficit (0 pages found).
- `NO-CHANGE`: Utility, policy, or terminal page appropriately configured.
