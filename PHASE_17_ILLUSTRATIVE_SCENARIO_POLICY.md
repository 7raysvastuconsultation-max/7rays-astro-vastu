## 1. PURPOSE & DISTINCTION

Websites offering architectural, financial, or consulting advisory often present composite case studies to explain complex diagnostic methods. If these composite models are not conspicuously disclosed, search engines, raters, and consumers can mistake them for real client reviews or guaranteed outcomes.

This policy establishes the mandatory rules governing all educational scenarios on `https://7raysastrovastu.com/`.

---

## 2. MANDATORY LABELING & ON-PAGE NOTICE RULES

Every educational scenario published under `/case-studies/` or `/insights/` must satisfy the following four requirements:

1. **Title Prefix:** The H1 headline and meta title must include an explicit descriptor, such as:  
   `Illustrative Vastu Assessment — [Topic]` or `Educational Scenario — [Topic]`.
2. **Prominent Notice Banner:** A standardized contextual banner must appear directly above the main article body before the reader encounters scenario details:
   > **Illustrative Assessment Scenario:** This walkthrough illustrates our on-site spatial audit methodology and deliverable scope. In compliance with client privacy and advertising truthfulness standards, this scenario is an educational composite that does not represent a specific client engagement, customer review, or guaranteed commercial outcome.
3. **Breadcrumb Transparency:** The breadcrumb hierarchy must clearly identify the parent category as _"Illustrative Scenarios"_ or _"Assessment Scenarios"_ rather than _"Client Success Stories"_.
4. **Structured Data Integrity:** Schema markup must use `Article` or `WebPage` types. It is strictly prohibited to apply `Review`, `AggregateRating`, or pseudo-client author properties to illustrative scenarios.
