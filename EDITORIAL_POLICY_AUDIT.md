## 2. EDITORIAL STANDARDS & PRINCIPLES

### 2.1. Fact vs. Tradition Distinction

- **Vedic Architecture (Vastu Shastra)**: Presented as an ancient spatial harmonization discipline based on solar geometry, natural ventilation, magnetic cardinal directions, and elemental balance (Pancha Tattva).
- **Vedic Astrology (Jyotish)**: Presented as a symbolic, interpretive tradition tracking planetary cycles and archetypes.
- **Scientific Claims**: No unsupported claims of modern empirical physics or quantum mechanics are permitted. Where modern environmental factors are involved (e.g. electromagnetic fields), they are measured using calibrated physical instruments (Gauss meters) rather than mystical speculation.

### 2.2. Health & Medical Boundaries

- Vastu and Astrology do not diagnose, treat, or cure any medical or psychological condition.
- Content must explicitly advise readers experiencing health or mental issues to consult qualified medical doctors and healthcare professionals.
- Prohibited words: "cure", "heal illness", "disease eradication", "miracle recovery".

### 2.3. Financial & Legal Boundaries

- Career and business astrology content must clarify that professional success requires hard work, skill competence, and market viability.
- Astrology consultations never replace chartered accountants, financial advisors, or legal counsel.
- Prohibited promises: "guaranteed wealth", "lottery numbers", "guaranteed promotion", "surefire investment".
