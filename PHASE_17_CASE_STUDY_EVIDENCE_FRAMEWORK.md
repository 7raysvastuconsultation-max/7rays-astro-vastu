## 1. PURPOSE & PRINCIPLES

In local SEO, E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness), and search quality evaluation, case studies provide proof of competence. However, search quality raters and regulatory bodies (ASCI, FTC) heavily penalize websites that present hypothetical scenarios, stock photography, or fictional personas as "real client results."

This framework defines the **strict evidentiary thresholds** required before any project can be designated, marketed, or published as a "Real Case Study" on `7raysastrovastu.com`.

---

## 2. THE 10-POINT EVIDENCE CRITERIA

To qualify as a genuine, non-fictional case study, a candidate project must satisfy all ten criteria:

1. **Explicit Client Consent:** Documented written or electronic consent from the property owner or authorized corporate representative permitting public case-study documentation.
2. **Real Engagement Context:** An actual, completed consultation booking with verifiable booking records, service type, and consultation mode (on-site or CAD remote).
3. **Verifiable Initial Spatial Condition:** Documented architectural parameters (e.g. 3BHK flat, commercial office floor plate, industrial warehouse) with verified directional alignment.
4. **Authentic Diagnostic Process:** Recorded on-site or virtual observations utilizing genuine diagnostic tools (calibrated digital compass, digital Gauss meter, or CAD 16-zone grid overlay).
5. **Concrete Site Constraints:** Identification of practical, non-ideal real-world limitations (e.g. shared plumbing shafts in apartments, leased commercial interior restrictions prohibiting civil demolition).
6. **Specific Non-Demolition Interventions:** Detailed, realistic remedial adjustments applied (e.g. copper/brass boundary inlays, room zoning swaps, elemental color balancing, workstation re-orientation).
7. **Documented Physical Execution:** Verification that the client or their interior contractors physically implemented the recommended spatial remedies.
8. **Client Privacy Protection:** Redaction of exact unit numbers, private financial statements, family disputes, and identifiable personal identifying information (PII).
9. **Empirical or Client-Reported Follow-Up:** A genuine post-consultation check-in (minimum 30 days post-implementation) recording real client feedback.
10. **Zero Guaranteed Causation:** Explicit framing that outcomes represent client-reported satisfaction and spatial balance, not scientific medical cures or guaranteed financial windfalls.
